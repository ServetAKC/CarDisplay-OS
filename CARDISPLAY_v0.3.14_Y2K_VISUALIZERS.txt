Car Display OS v0.3.14 - Y2K Green Visualizer Pack

Eight new brand-free green modes were added:

1. CYBER GRID - reactive skyline over a moving perspective grid
2. LASER TUNNEL - pulsing nested neon diamonds
3. DATA RAIN - audio-reactive falling data trails
4. NEON WAVE - bright CRT-style waveform with reflection
5. ORBIT LINK - rotating connected frequency nodes
6. PIXEL CITY - music-driven green city skyline
7. RADAR 2000 - rotating sweep with frequency blips
8. DUAL DISC - twin rotating audio decks

There are now 16 visualizer styles in total. Tap while the visualizer is open
to cycle modes. Every new mode repaints a fixed black canvas each frame to avoid
old pixels, particle trails and edge glitches.

OFFLINE PROJECT FIX
- Required CYD libraries are included in the lib folder.
- Internet library dependencies were removed from platformio.ini.
